The example glue code, tests, documentation and original illustrations in this
repository are MIT licensed. Basira, TSST and ODVL run in Basira Cloud; their
implementations are not included or licensed by this repository. The official
SDK is downloaded separately from Basira's public distribution, checked by
SHA-256 and kept outside Git. Its distribution and the service are governed by
their respective terms. No private engine, customer conversation, credential,
or Élya session checkpoint is bundled.

The offline demonstration uses invented fixtures and a test double. It does
not reproduce the liquid-memory engines or measure Cloud performance.
